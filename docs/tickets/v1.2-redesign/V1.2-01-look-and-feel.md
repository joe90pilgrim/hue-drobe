# V1.2-01 Look and feel foundations

**Ceiling:** 1.5 hours. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Haiku, high. Styles and a font only; no layout or behaviour changes.
**Depends on:** Version 1.1 done
**Design:** `docs/designs/2026-11-15-v1.2-redesign/brief.md`, section B1 ("Tokens" and "Colour accuracy rules"). Any screen in that folder shows the result.

## Goal
Give the app its new neutral colours, type and buttons, so the interface no longer changes how garment colours look, and the app feels less dull.

## Scope
- Replace the CSS variables in `app/src/style.css` with the tokens in the brief. Remove every use of the green accent (`--accent`, `--accent-deep`, `#eef3f0`, `#f6f8f7`).
- The avatar stage background becomes `#E3E3E3`.
- Chosen states (answers, segments, favourite tiles, neckline buttons) are shown in ink: a 2 px ink border, or a ring of 2 px surface then 2 px ink. No tints.
- Main buttons are ink with white text; secondary buttons are white with a `#D6D6D6` border.
- Every colour swatch gets the faint inner edge `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.1)`.
- Add Bricolage Grotesque (600 and 700) for headings, self-hosted as `woff2` in `app/public/fonts/` and cached by the service worker. Inter stays for body text.
- Raise text that is under 12 px to at least 12 px.

## Out of scope
Layout, navigation and wording. Those are V1.2-02 to V1.2-07. Existing screens will look half-finished until those land; that's expected.

## Decisions (made 15 Nov 2026, Joe)
- Keep the overall look; change to true neutral colours with ink as the only accent. No brand accent colour for now.
- New dependency: Bricolage Grotesque. **Check its licence before adding it** (it is listed under the SIL Open Font License on Google Fonts) and record it in the ticket's Done note, as `CLAUDE.md` asks.

## Acceptance criteria
- No green, beige or other tinted colour is left in `style.css`; the stage is exactly `#E3E3E3`; greys are true neutrals (equal red, green and blue).
- Headings use Bricolage Grotesque with the network off (the font is served by the app).
- No text below 12 px.
- `npm run check` passes.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the Pixel 8 and the laptop: nothing coloured in the interface sits next to the avatar or a swatch.

## Done (16 Nov 2026)
Joe checked it on the Pixel 8 and the laptop and signed it off. Ticket closed.
- **What changed:** `app/src/style.css` now uses the brief's neutral tokens with ink as the only accent (no green, beige or tint), the `#E3E3E3` stage, ink chosen states, ink and white buttons, the faint inner edge on every swatch, Bricolage Grotesque (600 and 700) for headings, and nothing under 12 px. Layout, wording and behaviour are unchanged. New checks: `app/tests/style.test.ts` (only neutral greys, stage colour, 12 px floor, swatch edge) and `app/e2e/look.spec.ts` (font loaded from this origin with no outside request, font cached by the service worker, stage colour, no text under 12 px on all three screens). `npm run check` passes (51 unit tests, 23 e2e).
- **Dependency: Bricolage Grotesque 5.3.0** (via the `@fontsource` build, latin subset, 600 and 700, about 22 KB each), self-hosted in `app/public/fonts/` with its licence `OFL.txt`. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1. It allows use, embedding and redistribution, including commercially, as long as the licence text travels with the font and the font is not sold on its own. It replaces nothing; it enables the headings. Downloaded once from cdn.jsdelivr.net with Joe's say-so; the app itself makes no network call for it.
- **Also touched:** `app/vite.config.ts` lists the two font files in the service worker precache. Two quiz specs (`quiz.spec.ts`, `favourites.spec.ts`) were already failing before this ticket, because V1.1-07's hidden close-second palette doubled the `.chip` and `.fav-toggle` counts; they now look only at the season's own palette. The favourite-tile gap went from 4 px to 8 px so the new 4 px ring does not touch the next tile.
- **Left for later tickets, not done here:** the browser `theme-color` (`#3E5C4E`) in `index.html`, the manifest colours and the app icons are still green or beige. They sit outside `style.css`; V1.2-02 (logo and shell) is the natural place. Tiles and buttons under 44 px (favourite tiles 28 px, neckline buttons 32 px) wait for V1.2-04.
