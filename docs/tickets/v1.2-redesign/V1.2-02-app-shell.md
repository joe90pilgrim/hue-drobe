# V1.2-02 App shell and navigation

**Ceiling:** 3 hours. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Sonnet, medium. Touches every screen's frame, the routes and the backup UI.
**Depends on:** V1.2-01
**Design:** `docs/designs/2026-11-15-v1.2-redesign/brief.md`, section B2. Screens: `00-map-of-screens.html`, `phone-01-try-on.html` (top bar and tabs), `phone-04-menu-back-up.html`, `desktop-01-try-on.html` (sidebar), `desktop-03-back-up.html`.

## Goal
Show the app's name and logo, rename the tabs to what they are, make the phone tab bar slimmer, and give back up and restore its own place, with room for the pages still to come.

## Scope
- **Phone (under 900 px):**
  - A 52 px top bar with the logo and "Hue-drobe" on the left and a Menu button on the right.
  - The bottom tab bar is 56 px plus the safe-area inset. Its tabs are **Try on**, **Wardrobe** and **My colours**, and the active one has an ink label and a grey pill behind its icon.
- **Laptop (900 px and wider):**
  - A 232 px sidebar with the logo and wordmark at the top, the three items, and "Back up and restore" at the foot.
  - Remove the 960 px `max-width` on the main area.
- **Page titles:** remove the per-screen `<header>` titles. Wardrobe and My colours get a page title inside their content (V1.2-06 and V1.2-07 style it); Try on has none.
- **Back up and restore:**
  - Move it out of the Wardrobe, into a bottom sheet from the Menu on a phone and a centred dialog on a laptop.
  - The buttons read "Save a backup" and "Restore from file", and the explanation reads "Restoring from a file replaces everything on this device."
  - Replace `confirm()` before restoring with an in-app confirmation (Replace and Cancel).
- **Routes:**
  - The new routes are `#/try-on`, `#/wardrobe` and `#/colours`.
  - `#/dress` and `#/colour` still work, by mapping to the new routes.
  - Update `tests/route.test.ts` and the end-to-end specs.

## Out of scope
The contents of each screen (V1.2-03 to V1.2-07). The person switcher (version 2) and a fourth tab: leave room, build nothing.

## Decisions (made 15 Nov 2026, Joe)
- The tab names are Try on, Wardrobe and My colours. "Imagine" and "Learn" are themes, not names on screen.
- Back up and restore lives in the Menu (phone) or at the foot of the sidebar (laptop); the version 2 person switcher goes there too.

## Acceptance criteria
- The name and logo show on every screen, on phone and laptop. **Fixes I11.**
- The tabs read Try on, Wardrobe and My colours; old links still open the right screen. **Fixes I9 (naming).**
- The phone tab bar is 56 px plus the safe area. **Fixes I14.**
- The Wardrobe has no backup card; back up and restore works from the Menu and the sidebar, including the round trip in `e2e/backup.spec.ts`. **Fixes I15.**
- Adding a fourth tab later would need no layout change (Joe checks against the map). **Fixes I13.**
- `npm run check` passes.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the Pixel 8 and the laptop.
