## Goal
Keep the Wardrobe easy to use as it fills up, and make it clear how to try on, rename or delete a piece.

## Scope
- The page title is "Wardrobe", with a two-part switch underneath: **Clothes · n** and **Outfits · n**, with live counts.
- **Clothes:**
  - Filter chips: **All**, **Tops · n** and **Bottoms · n**. Each garment's group comes from its slot, so new garment types sort themselves.
  - A grid of colour cards (name and kind): two columns on a phone, and as many as fit at 170 px or wider on a laptop.
- **Outfits:** the same grid. Each card shows stacked swatches (the top above a narrower bottom). A deleted piece shows as a dashed outline.
- **Tapping a card:**
  - On a phone it opens a sheet with **Try it on** (main button), **Rename** and **Delete**.
  - On a laptop it opens a panel to the right of the grid, 280 to 340 px wide, and the chosen card gets an ink border.
- **Actions:**
  - Rename edits the name in the sheet or panel; Enter saves and Escape cancels.
  - Delete asks first, in-app: "Delete Navy tee? Outfits that use it will show a gap where it was."
  - Try it on works as today: wear the item or outfit, then go to Try on.
- **When there's nothing yet:**
  - Clothes: "Nothing here yet. On Try on, change a piece and tap Add to wardrobe."
  - Outfits: "No outfits yet. On Try on, put a look together and tap Save outfit."
- No change to storage: `wardrobe.ts` and `outfits.ts` stay as they are.

## Out of scope
Search, sorting and more filters (roadmap). Undo after deleting (roadmap).

## Acceptance criteria
- With 20 saved clothes, the Clothes view fits several screens' worth into one and can be cut down to tops or bottoms. Test this with a seeded fixture, not Joe's data. **Fixes I7.**
- The rows no longer carry Rename and Delete; trying on, renaming and deleting are all reached by tapping a card, and Delete always asks first. **Fixes I8.**
- On the laptop the grid uses the width of the screen. **Fixes I18 (Wardrobe).**
- `npm run check` passes, with `e2e/wardrobe.spec.ts` and `e2e/outfits.spec.ts` updated.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the Pixel 8 and the laptop.
