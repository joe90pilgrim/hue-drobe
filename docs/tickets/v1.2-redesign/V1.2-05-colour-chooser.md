# V1.2-05 Colour chooser

**Ceiling:** 3 hours. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Sonnet, medium. A new component with colour conversion, live updates to the avatar and keyboard access.
**Depends on:** V1.2-04
**Design:** `docs/designs/2026-11-15-v1.2-redesign/brief.md`, section B5. Screens: `phone-03-try-on-choose-a-colour.html`, `desktop-02-try-on-choose-a-colour.html`. Both work: try them before starting.

## Goal
Replace the browser's colour picker, which on Android is a modal that covers the avatar, with a chooser inside the app's panel, so the avatar stays in view and changes as you choose.

## Scope
- A new `colour-chooser.ts`, opened from "Choose a colour" in V1.2-04.
  - On a phone it replaces the sheet's contents, with "‹ Top colour" (back) and "Done".
  - On a laptop it replaces the slot's controls inside the panel.
- **Contents, top to bottom:**
  - The chosen colour (48 px swatch).
  - **Colour code**, a text field showing the hex value; typing a valid six-digit code applies it.
  - The Favourite toggle.
  - **Favourites** (the same tiles as V1.2-04).
  - **Basics:** `#FFFFFF`, `#F2EDE3`, `#C8B89A`, `#8C8C8C`, `#1A1A1A` and `#5B4636`.
  - **Three labelled range sliders:**
    - **Colour** (hue 0 to 359).
    - **Strength** (saturation 0 to 100), with "greyer to richer" on the right.
    - **Lightness** (HSV value 0 to 100), with "darker to lighter" on the right.
- Each slider's track shows its range for the current colour, as in the design files. Thumbs are white with a 3 px ink border.
- The avatar updates live as a slider moves (`viewer.setColour` on `input`). Save to storage when the slider is released or the chooser closes, as today's `change` handler does.
- Use the same `hsvToHex` and `hexToHsv` maths as the design files, and add unit tests for both, including a round trip of the Basics and the True Winter palette.
- Remove every `<input type="color">` from the app.

## Out of scope
Colour names, eyedroppers, or matching from photos (version 4).

## Decisions (made 15 Nov 2026, Joe)
- The built-in chooser is in 1.2: the current one covers the avatar on the phone and is hard to use.
- Keep the Colour code field.

## Acceptance criteria
- On the Pixel 8, choosing a colour never covers the avatar, and the avatar changes as a slider moves. **Fixes I4.**
- All text in the chooser wraps; nothing is cut off at 390 px wide.
- The sliders work with the keyboard on the laptop, and each has a visible label.
- A colour chosen here is exactly the colour applied to the garment and saved (same hex), checked by unit tests.
- `npm run check` passes.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the Pixel 8 and the laptop.

## Built (16 Nov 2026), waiting for Joe's sign-off
`npm run check` passes (61 unit tests, 40 e2e). Not yet checked on the Pixel 8 or the laptop by Joe.
- **What changed:** `colour-chooser.ts` is the new chooser and `colour-maths.ts` holds `hsvToHex` and `hexToHsv`. "Choose a colour" in the change-a-piece panel now swaps the panel's controls for the chooser (phone sheet and laptop panel alike). The header becomes "‹ Top colour" (back) and Done. Contents are as the ticket lists: swatch, Colour code, Favourite, Favourites (first 12, Show all), Basics, and the Colour, Strength and Lightness sliders with the tracks from the design.
- **Live and saved:** a slider calls `viewer.setColour` on `input` and saves on release; a typed code, a tile or a Basics colour applies and saves at once. Closing the chooser saves too. Every `<input type="color">` is gone.
- **Checked at 390 px (browser pane, not the Pixel 8):** the avatar stage stays about 234 px high and visible, and nothing overflows sideways. The e2e test checks the stage stays above 120 px and the tiles are 44 px.
- **Round trip:** HSV is held as whole numbers, so hex to HSV to hex can move a channel by up to 3 steps for the Basics and True Winter palette (the test sets that limit). The chooser never converts a colour it is given: a code, tile or favourite is applied and saved as the exact hex, and only moving a slider produces a new one.
- **Judgement calls:** Escape in the chooser goes back to the controls first, then closes the panel. The browser Back gesture closes the whole panel. If there are no favourites the Favourites block is hidden.
- **Tests:** `e2e/colour-chooser.spec.ts` is new; the other specs use `setColour` and `expectColour` in `e2e/helpers.ts`. The favourites 13-colour test now stays in the chooser, as it was too slow for its 30 s limit otherwise. Some e2e tests failed once or twice in full runs under load (a palette colour after reload; the 13-colour test), and the palette one also failed on the unchanged code, so the suite is sensitive to load.
