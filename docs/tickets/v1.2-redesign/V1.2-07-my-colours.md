## Goal
Make clear that this part of the app is a quiz and its results, put the quiz buttons within thumb reach, and make the palette smaller and quicker to favourite from.

## Scope
- **Before the quiz:**
  - The title is "My colours". A card reads "Find the colours that suit you", then "Answer {n} short questions about your skin, hair and eyes. You'll get your colour season, a palette of colours to wear, and some general advice."
  - A tip box reads "Do it in daylight, by a window rather than under a lamp, with a mirror to hand."
  - Then the **Take the quiz** button, at the bottom of the screen on a phone.
- **A question:**
  - "Colour quiz · question 3 of 11", an ink progress bar, and the question in Bricolage.
  - Answers are 52 px rows; the chosen one has a 2 px ink border and a tick.
  - **Back** and **Next** are pinned to the bottom on a phone (one third and two thirds of the width). Next stays disabled until there's an answer, and the last question's button reads "See my result".
- **Your result:**
  - A card with the season name and description, then the close-second block from V1.1-07 (same behaviour, styled as a bordered box).
  - A **Your palette** card: swatches are 44 px on a phone and 52 px on a laptop, and each swatch is itself the favourite toggle.
    - A favourite shows a white heart badge, and the line "♥ n in your favourites" sits underneath.
    - The helper text reads "Tap a colour to add it to your favourites. Favourites are offered when you choose a garment colour."
  - Then the three advice sections and **Retake the quiz** (secondary).
  - Use the same swatch component in the close-second palette.
- **Laptop:** the intro and the questions sit in a centred column up to 640 px wide. The result uses two columns: the season and palette on the left, the advice on the right.
- No change to `quiz/quiz.ts`, its scoring or the stored result.

## Acceptance criteria
- The screen and tab never call the quiz just "Colour". **Fixes I9.**
- The palette has no Save buttons, and favouriting works by tapping a swatch, both for your season and for the close second. **Fixes I10.**
- On the Pixel 8, Back and Next are reachable with one thumb, and the chosen answer is shown in ink, not green. **Fixes I19.**
- On the laptop the result fills the width in two columns. **Fixes I18 (My colours).**
- `npm run check` passes, with `e2e/quiz.spec.ts` and `e2e/favourites.spec.ts` updated.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the Pixel 8 and the laptop.
