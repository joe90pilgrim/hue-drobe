# V1.2-03 Try on layout

**Ceiling:** 3 hours. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Sonnet, medium. A new layout for the main screen on phone and laptop, built from the slot list so it can grow.
**Depends on:** V1.2-02
**Design:** `docs/designs/2026-11-15-v1.2-redesign/brief.md`, section B3. Screens: `phone-01-try-on.html`, `desktop-01-try-on.html`.

## Goal
Make the avatar the centre of the Try on screen, with a short list of what you're wearing that can grow to more garment types and layers.

## Scope
- Rename the Dress screen to Try on (`screens/dress.ts` to `screens/try-on.ts` if useful).
- **Phone:**
  - The stage fills the full width and the remaining height.
  - Under it is the "What you're wearing" card, with one row per slot. Each row is a single button at least 64 px high, holding the swatch, a small slot label, the garment name ("T-shirt, crew neck", "Trousers" or "Nothing") and "Change ›".
  - Then the full-width **Save outfit** main button.
- **Laptop:** the stage on the left fills the height; a panel on the right, 360 to 420 px wide, holds the rows and Save outfit.
- Build the rows from the `SLOTS` list, so a new slot adds a row with no layout change.
- In this ticket, a row's "Change" can keep today's controls, moved into a simple expanding area under the row. V1.2-04 replaces them.
- **Stage hint:** "Drag to turn · pinch to zoom" on a phone, or "Drag to turn · scroll to zoom" on a laptop. Hide it after the first drag.
- **Status text:**
  - While loading: "Getting your avatar ready…".
  - On failure: "The avatar couldn't load. Try reloading the page."
  - Show the developer instruction only with `?debug`.

## Out of scope
The contents of the panel for changing a piece (V1.2-04) and the colour chooser (V1.2-05). Viewer lighting (roadmap).

## Acceptance criteria
- On the Pixel 8 the avatar uses the full width of the screen. **Fixes I1.**
- Adding a third entry to `SLOTS` in a scratch branch adds a third row with no other change (check, then discard). **Fixes I5.**
- No developer instruction shows to the user without `?debug`. **Fixes I16.**
- On the laptop the stage and panel fill the screen at 1280 × 800. **Fixes I18 (Try on).**
- Save outfit behaves as before (disabled with nothing worn, "Outfit saved" for 1.5 seconds).
- `npm run check` passes.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the Pixel 8 and the laptop, including the frame rate staying comfortable.
