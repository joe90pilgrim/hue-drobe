# V1.2-04 Change a piece

**Ceiling:** 3 hours. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Sonnet, medium. A bottom sheet and a panel with several controls, with focus handling and the existing favourites and saving logic.
**Depends on:** V1.2-03
**Design:** `docs/designs/2026-11-15-v1.2-redesign/brief.md`, section B4. Screens: `phone-02-try-on-change-a-piece.html`, `desktop-01-try-on.html` (the open Top section).

## Goal
Change one piece at a time in a clear top-to-bottom order (garment, neckline, colour), with one plainly named action for each kind of saving.

## Scope
- **Phone:**
  - Tapping a row opens a bottom sheet for that slot. The stage shrinks to the space above the sheet; it is not covered.
  - Done, the back gesture or a tap on the stage closes the sheet.
  - It's a dialog: focus moves into it and returns to the row when it closes.
- **Laptop:** the same controls open inside the right-hand panel, in place of the row.
- **Contents, top to bottom:**
  1. **Garment:** a segmented control with T-shirt, Sweatshirt and **Nothing** ("None" is renamed).
  2. **Neckline:** a segmented control with Crew, V-neck and Scoop, shown only for the T-shirt.
  3. **Colour:**
     - The current colour as a 48 px swatch, then "Choose a colour" (opens the browser's picker until V1.2-05 lands), then a **Favourite** toggle (a heart and the word, with `aria-pressed`).
     - Below that, the favourite tiles at 44 px, with the white dot on palette colours and the legend "From your colour palette".
     - Show the first 12, then "Show all".
  4. **Add to wardrobe:** a full-width secondary button. After saving it reads "In your wardrobe" with a tick until something changes. It's hidden when the garment is "Nothing".
- Remove the per-slot star, Edit, More, Save and arrow buttons.
- To remove a favourite, choose it and turn Favourite off.
- Keep the storage and logic as they are (`favourites.ts`, `ensureItem`, the neckline key).

## Out of scope
The built-in colour chooser (V1.2-05).

## Decisions (made 15 Nov 2026, Joe)
- One named action per kind of saving: **Favourite** (a colour), **Add to wardrobe** (a garment in a colour), **Save outfit** (the whole look).
- The favourites limit and "show more" behaviour stay as decided in V1.1-01: the first 12, then more.

## Acceptance criteria
- Joe can change the garment, neckline and colour of the top without hunting: the controls read top to bottom in that order. **Fixes I2.**
- The word "Save" appears only in "Save outfit" on Try on. **Fixes I3.**
- Every control in the sheet is at least 44 px on the phone. **Fixes I17 (Try on).**
- Favourites, saved clothes and the neckline survive a reload, as before.
- `npm run check` passes, with `e2e/favourites.spec.ts`, `e2e/wardrobe.spec.ts`, `e2e/necklines.spec.ts`, `e2e/shorts.spec.ts` and `e2e/outfits.spec.ts` updated to the new controls.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the Pixel 8 and the laptop.
