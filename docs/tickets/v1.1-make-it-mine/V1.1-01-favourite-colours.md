# V1.1-01 Favourite colours in the picker

**Ceiling:** 1 hour. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Haiku, high. Small UI work on screens that already exist.
**Depends on:** MVP quiz and colour picker

## Goal
From the quiz result, save colours from your palette as favourites; they appear at the top of the colour picker.

## Scope
- A 'save to favourites' control on each swatch of the quiz result palette.
- Favourites shown as swatches in the colour picker, above the free picker; tap one to apply it.
- Remove a favourite from either place.
- Stored on the device (IndexedDB), like the rest of the MVP data.

## Decisions needed from Joe
- Can favourites include colours from outside your season's palette (for example, picked freely in the colour picker)? Recommendation: yes, but palette colours are marked as such.
- Is there a limit on the number of favourites? Recommendation: no limit, but show the first 12 and a 'more' option.

If a decision isn't made when this ticket starts, stop and ask rather than guess.

## Acceptance criteria
- A colour saved from the quiz result appears in the colour picker and applies to the selected garment.
- Favourites survive closing and reopening the app.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the quiz result and picker on phone and desktop.
