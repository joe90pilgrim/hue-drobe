# V1.1-01 Favourite colours in the picker

**Ceiling:** 1 hour. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Haiku, high. Small UI work on screens that already exist.
**Depends on:** MVP quiz and colour picker

## Goal
From the quiz result, save colours from your palette as favourites; they appear at the top of the colour picker.

## Scope
- A 'save to favourites' control on each swatch of the quiz result palette.
- Favourites shown as swatches in the colour picker, above the free picker; tap one to apply it.
- Remove a favourite from either place.
- Stored on the device (IndexedDB), like the rest of the MVP data.

## Decisions (decided 15 Nov 2026, Joe)
- **Off-palette colours:** yes. Colours picked freely in the colour picker can be saved too; palette colours are marked as such. See `docs/decisions/2026-11-15-v1.1-01-favourite-decisions.md`.
- **Limit:** no limit. The picker shows the first 12 favourites and a 'more' option.

## Acceptance criteria
- A colour saved from the quiz result appears in the colour picker and applies to the selected garment.
- Favourites survive closing and reopening the app.
- Joe's visual sign-off on the quiz result and picker on phone and desktop.

## Done (15 Nov 2026), awaiting Joe's sign-off
Each palette swatch on the quiz result has a Save/Saved button. Each slot in the Dress screen shows the saved favourites above its colour picker: tap one to apply it, the star saves or removes the colour the picker shows, and Edit removes others. Palette colours carry a dot. The first 12 show, then More. Stored in IndexedDB under `favourites` (`app/src/favourites.ts`). Decisions: `docs/decisions/2026-11-15-v1.1-01-favourite-decisions.md`.
Checks: `npm run check` passes (typecheck, 21 unit tests, asset and deploy checks, 7 e2e tests including 3 new in `app/e2e/favourites.spec.ts`). Checked in the dev server at phone and desktop widths.
