# V1.1-04 Other t-shirt necklines

**Ceiling:** 1.5 hours. The most this ticket may take, not a target. Stop as soon as the acceptance criteria are met.
**Worker:** Sonnet, high. New cut shapes in the Blender garment code, but small variations on the crew neck that already works. Escalate to Opus only on three strikes.
**Depends on:** MVP avatar pack build

## Goal
Offer more tee necklines alongside the crew neck.

## Scope
- Add neckline variants to the tee template as different neck cut shapes on the same offset shell.
- Each variant goes through the same hard mesh checks as the crew neck, on Joe and the six synthetic bodies.
- Neckline becomes a choice when choosing the tee and is stored on saved items (V1.1-02).

## Decisions needed from Joe
- **Which necklines?** Suggested: V-neck and scoop. Others such as henley (buttons) or polo (collar) are new geometry and would move to v3.
- How deep should the V and the scoop be? A measurement in cm below the crew neck, or 'match a tee I own'.

If a decision isn't made when this ticket starts, stop and ask rather than guess.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each neckline passes the hard mesh checks on all bodies.
- Joe can choose a neckline in the app, and it is remembered on saved items.
- Joe's visual sign-off: each reads as that neckline from the front and side.

## Decisions (made 15 Nov 2026, see `docs/decisions/2026-11-15-v1.1-04-neckline-decisions.md`)
- V-neck and scoop. Henley and polo stay in v3.
- V-neck 12 cm and scoop 9 cm below the shoulder-top line (a measurement, not 'match a tee I own').

## Status
Built, waiting for Joe's visual sign-off. The Dress screen shows Crew, V-neck and Scoop buttons when the T-shirt is selected; the choice is remembered, kept on saved tees (record version 2, old tees load as crew) and on outfits. `npm run check` passes (17 end-to-end tests, 4 of them new).

Measured by `python pack/build_pack.py --private --reports-to pack/reports`, on Joe and the six synthetic bodies (seven in all):
- Hard mesh checks, V and scoop, every body: 1 piece, 4 openings, 0 body-through-cloth pairs, 0 self-intersecting pairs, 0 through the trousers, minimum clearance 0.3 cm (limit 0.2), shoulder gap 0.41 to 0.46 cm (limit 0.5).
- Front depth below the shoulder-top line: V 11.6 to 12.4 cm (asked 12), scoop 8.8 to 9.5 cm (asked 9). Joe: V 11.8, scoop 8.8.
- Neckline width within 0.4 cm of the config on every body.
- The crew tee's file is byte-identical to before (Joe's SHA-256 unchanged), so existing looks do not change.

Things to know:
- The crew neck is set at 6 cm but measures 6.3 to 7.4 cm on the built meshes (7.4 on Joe), because the cut edge is smoothed. So the scoop is only about 1.5 cm deeper than the crew neck; it differs more in width (10% wider) and shape. If it should read as deeper, raise `tee.necklines.scoop.drop_front_cm` in `pack/fit_config.json`.
- The V's lower edge is slightly wavy close to the tip when zoomed right in. The cut removes whole mesh faces; the edge is projected onto the V line, but a clean tip would need a finer mesh near the neck.
- Not done: look at the V and scoop from the front and side on your phone (frame rate and download size: pack is 969 KB, up from 850 KB).
