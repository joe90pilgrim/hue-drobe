## Goal
When the quiz result is borderline, also show the season you are close to (from the MVP backlog).

## Scope
- Show 'you are also close to X' on the result screen when confidence is below a threshold.
- Optionally let you view the second season's palette and advice.

If a decision isn't made when this ticket starts, stop and ask rather than guess.
