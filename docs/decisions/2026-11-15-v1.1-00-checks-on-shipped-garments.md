# V1.1-00: checks now validate the garments the app ships

Date: 2026-11-15. Ticket: `docs/tickets/v1.1-make-it-mine/V1.1-00-checks-on-shipped-garments.md`.

## Decision

`spike/fixtures/reports/` is removed. `npm run check` now validates `pack/reports/`: the reports `build_pack.py` makes from `pack/fit_config.json` on the six synthetic bodies. Joe's go-ahead to replace the old reports was given when he asked for the ticket to start (the ticket's recommendation was yes).

## Why

The old reports were built with `--drape` and real-item flat measurements. They passed, but they described garments the app does not ship, so a bad fit config could not fail the check.

## What changed

- Old reports deleted. Their three known exceptions in `spike/known_exceptions.json` (two B4 trouser calibrations, one 0.01 cm shoulder gap) are removed with them; the file is now empty. The new reports pass with no exceptions. Tolerances in `spike/tolerances.json` are unchanged.
- `build_pack.py` gains `--config FILE` (try a fit without editing the committed file) and `--reports-to DIR` (copy the checked synthetic reports, committed config only, all six bodies).
- Each report is stamped with the SHA-256 of its fit config. `pack/test_reports.py` fails if `fit_config.json` changes without the reports being rebuilt.
- `check-assets.mjs` runs the checker on `pack/reports/` with `--sweatshirt`, so a missing sweatshirt fails.

## Evidence

- Full build of the six bodies from the committed config: all checks passed, 0 failures, no KNOWN items.
- A config with tee `chest_ease_cm` 30 on `synthetic_small`: 3 failures (30 self-intersecting face pairs, calibration not converged, chest error -5.2 cm against 2.0), no pack written, exit 1.
