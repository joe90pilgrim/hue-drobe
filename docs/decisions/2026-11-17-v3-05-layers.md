# V3-05: Multiple top layers

Date: 17 Nov 2026. Status: **stopped, not finished.** The pack build makes the layered tops and the layer check runs, but 12 checks fail by about 1 mm at one spot on each shoulder. Nothing is in the app yet. Work is on branch `feat/V3-05-layers`, not master, because the committed build fails its own checks.

## Decisions (Joe, 17 Nov 2026)
- **Three layers at most:** base (tee), mid (shirt, sweatshirt), outer (jacket, V3-04).
- **Combinations built and checked:** tee under shirt (three sleeve lengths) and tee under sweatshirt. **Not** shirt under sweatshirt, so tee + shirt + sweatshirt is not offered. Jackets are added in V3-04.

## What was built
- `garment.build_tee` takes `inner_mesh` (a mesh or a list of meshes) and `inner_gap`: the new top is kept `inner_gap` off the tee vertex for vertex along the body normal (both shells come from the same body vertices) and by nearest point, and its hem and cuff bands follow the tee where that is nearer.
- The generator builds four more meshes per body at the ease and sleeve length each top was calibrated to on its own, over the crew, V and scoop tees together (so one mesh clears all three necklines): `sweatshirt_over_tee`, `shirt_short_over_tee`, `shirt_mid_over_tee`, `shirt_long_over_tee`. Config: `pack/fit_config.json` `layers.gap_cm` 0.4. The pack lists them in `build_pack.py` `OVER_TEE` and the manifest.
- Checks: each layered top gets the tee's hard mesh limits, shading, bust-shape and against-trousers, shorts and tights limits (`check_reports.py --layers`), plus the layer check proper (`mesh_checks.layers`, report key `layers`, limits in `spike/tolerances.json` `layers`): no face pairs with the tee in any neckline, and every tee vertex at least 0.2 cm inside the outer top.
- Proposed, for Joe to agree: `layers.inner_clearance_cm_min` 0.2, `layers.shoulder_gap_extra_cm` 0.6 (the outer top's shoulder may stand that much further off the body, because the tee is under it; measured 0.9 to 1.0 cm band mean against 0.5 for the tee alone).

## What was tried at the shoulder (side of the shoulder, about 130 cm up on this body)
1. Nearest-point push from the tee's mesh only: tee vertices 0.05 to 0.18 cm inside; the V and scoop tees also crossed the sweatshirt (20 pairs), so the top is now built over all three together.
2. A final pass raising the nearest outer face along its own normal until each tee vertex was 2.5 mm inside: it ran away (5000 body pairs); with a largest-request-only rule it still tilted the cloth at the shoulder (up to 70 body pairs, one shirt 9 cm too wide).
3. The same on triangles cut as the exported file cuts them (first to third corner): the shoulder is judged right, but the armpit folds.
4. Raising it along the body normal: body pairs and self-intersections (28 and 79) at the underarm; limited to up-facing cloth it does nothing at this spot, which faces sideways.
5. A bigger gap (0.6 cm): the same spot gets worse (-0.62) and the underarm self-intersects (12 pairs).

I believe the cause is the crease between the arm and the shoulder in the A-pose: the two shells are offset from the same body, and where the body curves away the outer one cannot clear the inner one by 2 mm without folding. Not proven.

## Second pass (Claude Opus, 17 Nov 2026): the shoulder spot was the check, not the cloth; four bodies fail for real
- **The 1 mm shoulder failure was a measuring error.** At the failing tee vertices (synthetic medium man, x about 19 cm, 130 cm up) the true distance to the outer top is 0.40 to 0.44 cm, and a ray straight out along the tee's normal meets the outer cloth from the inside at 0.43 to 0.48 cm. The check signed the distance by the nearest face's normal; in the arm-shoulder crease the nearest point is a corner (0.05 cm from it) whose face lies side-on (normal against the tee's -0.09 to -0.22), which gave the wrong sign. `mesh_checks.layers` now uses the nearest face's normal only when the nearest point is inside that face, and otherwise decides inside or outside by that ray and reports the true distance. The 0.2 cm limit is unchanged. Diagnostic: per-vertex signed and unsigned distance, corner distance and ray hit, run on `pack/out` in Blender 5.3.
- **Result, all six synthetic bodies (`pack/build_pack.py --jobs 3`):** synthetic medium and small men pass every check. The other four fail (88 checks), and these are real failures, not the check: on female small and female large the sweatshirt and shirts cross the tee (20 to 22 face pairs per tee) and self-intersect (2 to 21 pairs at 125 to 135 cm); on large man and female medium the tee comes out through the top by up to 0.64 cm (tightest at the side of the shoulder, about 127 to 137 cm up, and on the women also under the bust at about 112 cm); the sweatshirt over the tee on female medium misses the bust-shape limits (hollow below 0.77 and 0.9 cm, max 0.7). No reports were refreshed, because the build fails its checks.
- **Not started:** the app (it needs a new optional `over` field on saved outfits, which is a data-model change for Joe to agree), phone frame rate, Joe's sign-off.
- **Next, for Joe to choose:** (a) build the over-tee shell from the tee itself (offset the tee's own vertices outward along the tee's normals, then cut and band as now), rather than from the body with the tee as an obstacle, so the order of layers holds by construction; or (b) let the layered top use cloth simulation over the tee (`spike/cloth/`, as skirts and coats do). (a) is the smaller change.

## Third pass (Claude Opus, 17 Nov 2026): cloth settle over the tee (Joe chose cloth simulation)
- **What was built:** `drape.settle_over` runs after each layered top is built. It is Blender cloth with gravity off and the shell's own edge lengths as rest lengths, with the tee in all three necklines, the body and the trousers as colliders (the tee: 0.4 cm outside, 1 cm inside thickness). Cloth that already clears the tee is pinned, and it is free within 3 cm of any spot where the tee is uncovered or nearer than 0.3 cm (`LAYER_SETTLE_FREE_CM`). Self-collision is on (`LAYER_SETTLE_NO_SELF=1` turns it off, for diagnosis only). 30 frames (`LAYER_SETTLE_FRAMES`).
- **Female large (was 28 failures):** 17 failures. Face pairs with the tee went from 20 to 0, and most cloth does not move (median 0, 99th percentile 0.6 cm). Still failing: on every top the tee is uncovered by up to 1.05 cm at the armpit crease (x about +/-17.9 cm, 127.4 cm up; a ray out from the tee hits no cloth), the shirts self-intersect at 125 cm (7 to 21 pairs), and the sweatshirt misses one bust-shape limit. Without self-collision: 16 failures, but 64 to 81 self-intersecting pairs.
- **Unpinned settle (first try):** no tee crossings, but cloth moved up to 4.8 cm, the armpit was uncovered by 1.0 to 1.15 cm, and the shoulder gap and self-intersections got worse.
- **Medium man:** still passes every check with the pinned settle. The other four bodies were not rebuilt with it.
- **Where it stands:** the armpit crease in the A-pose defeats every method tried (eight in all). The shell the cloth starts from already folds there, and with gravity off the solver only resolves contacts, so it cannot unfold it. Stopped under the ticket's three-attempts rule.
- **Options:** (a) for layered tops, start from the tee itself (offset the tee's own vertices outward), so the starting shell does not fold at the armpit, then use this settle for what is left; (b) drape the layered top with gravity and a garment-sized rest shape, as `drape.drape_tee` does, which is a full cloth fit (slower and not repeatable bit for bit); (c) accept a lower armpit limit, or exclude the underarm from the layer check, because it is hidden in the A-pose. Option (c) would change a tolerance and is Joe's call.
