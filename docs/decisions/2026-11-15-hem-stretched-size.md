# Tee circumference is judged against its stretched size where it sits on something larger

Date: 2026-11-15. Joe's decision, answering decision 1 in `2026-11-15-task5-tee-drape.md`.

## Decided

Task 5's exit number "tee circumference within 2 cm of the garment's own at waist and hem" compares the tee with the **larger** of:

- the garment's own circumference at that height (chest to hem, straight between), and
- the stretched size: the girth of what the tee sits on there (body and trousers together, measured round their outline, so the tee bridges the gap between the legs and over hollows) plus the cloth's 3 mm clearance all round.

So a tee whose hem is smaller than the jeans it covers is expected to stretch to the jeans, not to stay at its flat size.

## Why

Joe's tee hem is 94 cm round, his jeans about 98 cm where the hem sits. The real tee stretches over them, so its hem measures near the jeans' girth when worn. Comparing with the flat 94 cm would fail a correct result.
