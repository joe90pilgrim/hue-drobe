# Cloth settle and inner-layer hiding in fit_template.py; loose fit removed (V3.1-01)

Date: 18 November 2026. Ticket: `docs/tickets/v3.1-templates/V3.1-01-fold-experiment-into-fit-template.md`. Blender 5.3.0, MPFB 2.0.18, one thread.

## Decided

- The V3-05 to V3-14 branch chain was merged into `master` in one merge (Joe, 18 Nov), then the frozen V3-07b code was removed on top, so
  the history keeps both.
- `pack/fit_template.py` has the two techniques from the V3-14 experiment as meta options:
  - `settle`: Blender cloth from the MHCLO fit, pinned on the garment itself (`settle.pin`: front and back bands across the shoulders,
    optional cuffs, chosen on the template's own `.obj`), colliding with the body, `settle.collide_with` and the inner layers (grown out
    from the skin). `settle.rest: "template"` makes the template as made the cloth's rest shape (dynamic base mesh), as V3-15/V3-16.
  - `hide_inner` (default for a mid or outer layer with `inner_layers`): the inner layer's covered faces are removed and the rest is
    exported as `<name>_<inner>_under_<pack_file>.glb`. The layer check runs against that. `build_pack.py` copies each file into the pack
    and lists it in `manifest.json` under `garments.inner_layers_drawn`. Drawing it is V3.1-02.
- Removed: `loose` and its helpers, `inner_push`, the Shrinkwrap push off inner layers, and `settle.pinned`, `pin_above_armpit_cm`,
  `pin_small_pieces` and `gravity_ramp`. `validate_meta` refuses each one by name. `keep_islands` stays.
- The V3-09 blazer templates are retired (deleted).
- Known limit, accepted by Joe: the shirt still shows a crease across the bust and 5 body pairs at the cuffs on the large female body.
  Each template is judged from its renders.
