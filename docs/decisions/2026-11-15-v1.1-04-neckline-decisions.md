# V1.1-04: V-neck and scoop tees, 12 cm and 9 cm deep

Date: 2026-11-15. Ticket: `docs/tickets/v1.1-make-it-mine/V1.1-04-necklines.md`.

## Decisions (Joe, 15 Nov 2026)

- **Which necklines:** V-neck and scoop, alongside the crew neck. Henley (buttons) and polo (collar) are new geometry and stay in v3.
- **How deep:** a measurement below the shoulder-top line, not 'match a tee I own'. V-neck 12 cm, scoop 9 cm. The crew neck is set at 6 cm. These are set in `pack/fit_config.json` under `tee.necklines`, so changing one is a reviewed edit.

## What I chose without asking (shout if you disagree)

- **Width:** the V is as wide at the shoulders as the crew neck. The scoop is 10% wider (`width_factor` 1.1), because a scoop reads as wider as well as deeper.
- **Shape:** the back neckline is the crew curve for all three (a V-shaped back was a reported fault, B-log). Only the front changes. The V is two straight lines to the front centre; the scoop is a rounder, flatter-bottomed curve than the crew's ellipse.
- **One tee shell, three cuts:** each variant has the same ease, sleeve and hem as the crew tee (taken from the crew tee's calibration), so only the neck differs. The crew tee file is byte-identical to the one built before this ticket (same SHA-256).
- **Separate files:** `tee_v.glb` and `tee_scoop.glb` sit beside `tee.glb` in the pack (about 70 KB each). The app swaps which one is visible; the colour belongs to the tee, so it does not change when the neckline does.

## Data model change (the ticket asked for it; recorded because CLAUDE.md asks)

- A saved item is now `v: 2` with an optional `neckline` (`crew`, `v` or `scoop`), set on tees only. `cleanItems` upgrades version 1 records on load: a tee becomes a crew neck, so nothing already saved changes.
- A tee in the same colour with a different neckline is a different saved item. Auto-names: 'Navy tee' (crew, unchanged), 'Navy V-neck tee', 'Navy scoop-neck tee'.
- The Dress screen's current neckline is stored in IndexedDB under `neckline`. Wearing a saved tee or an outfit sets it. Outfits keep pointing at saved item ids, so they pick up the neckline through the item; no outfit record changed.
- The sweatshirt has no neckline choice. It keeps its own crew neck.

## Checks

- The V and scoop tees go through the same hard mesh limits as the crew tee (`spike/tolerances.json`, `garment_mesh`): one piece, four openings, no body through cloth, no self-intersection, none through the trousers, minimum clearance, shoulder gap. `spike/check_reports.py --necklines` fails the pack build if any is missing or missed.
- Each variant's chest, sleeve, neckline width and front depth are also compared with the fit config, within the garment landmark tolerance.
- The measured front depth is lower than the set depth because the cut is smoothed along its edge: see the ticket's status for the measured numbers on every body.
