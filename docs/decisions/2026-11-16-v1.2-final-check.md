# V1.2 final check against the designs (16 Nov 2026)

**Ticket:** V1.2-08. **Designs:** `docs/designs/2026-11-15-v1.2-redesign/` (phone at 390 × 844, laptop at 1280 × 800).

Decided while checking the built app against the designs. Each is a small change: wording, type sizes or the order of a list.

- **Deleted pieces read "top deleted" and "bottom deleted"**, as in the design (they read "Top missing" and "Bottom missing"). The gap test in `app/e2e/outfits.spec.ts` now expects the new text.
- **Colour chooser order** follows the brief (B5): colour code and Favourite first, then Favourites, Basics and the three sliders. The "Custom" heading goes.
- **Type sizes follow the brief's scale.** The quiz question is 24 px (30 px on a laptop) and the season name 34 px (40 px on a laptop). Before, the heading rule overrode both, so the laptop season was 24 px. Body text is 15 px and the advice headings 17 px.
- **Phone advice sits on the page**, without a card, as in the design. The laptop keeps its card.
- **Screenshots** come from `app/scripts/screenshots.mjs`, which seeds its own test browser with sample content, so the committed images do not depend on what is on a device. The stage's "couldn't load" line is hidden in them, because the avatar pack is blocked on purpose.

Bigger differences are for Joe to decide, and are listed in `docs/tickets/backlog.md` under V1.2-08.
