# V2-09 B's bust is smaller: an A cup, not MPFB's average

Date: 17 November 2026. Result: **B's bare bust projects 1.2 cm in front of her chest wall instead of 3.2 cm, her tops follow it, and the bust stays where it was.** Her chest is still 91.42 cm against the tape's 91.44 cm, every hard mesh check and every `bust_shape` limit passes, and `npm run check` passes (83 unit tests, 60 end-to-end tests). Not done: B's own visual sign-off (not until V6), and Joe's eye on whether 0.1 is the right cup value.

## What was wrong

B's body was built with MPFB's default cup (`cupsize` 0.5, "average"), which is not an A cup. Measured on the exported body (`pack/bust_shape.py`, `body_cup`):

| B's body | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cupsize` macro | 0.5 (default, never set) | 0.1 |
| Bust minus underbust (circumference at the bust line less 5 cm below it; about 2.5 cm is an A, 5 a B, 7.5 a C, 12.5 a DD) | 12.9 cm | 9.9 cm |
| Projection of each breast in front of the chest wall (apex depth less the mean depth 6 cm above and below it) | 3.2 cm | 1.2 cm |
| Bust line and apex height | 110.5 cm, apex 110.5 cm | 110.5 cm, apex 109.0 cm |
| Chest against the tape (tolerance 2 cm) | 91.43 cm (-0.01) | 91.42 cm (-0.02) |
| Waist, hips, height | pass | pass (0.05 cm, -0.05 cm, 0.0 cm) |

The tee that hangs from the bust (V2-08) follows the body, so it is smaller too: its apex is 17.7 cm deep against 18.6 cm, and it drops 0.4 cm above the apex against 0.7 cm. Joe's report ("the bust is too big and too long, the position is right") matches this: the V2-08 hang spread whatever the body's bust was down the front.

## What changed

- **B's cup.** First set as `"cupsize": 0.1` in her `shape` block (the V2-02 input, already documented). 0 is the smallest cup MPFB can make and looks nearly flat from the side, so 0.1 was chosen by eye. Joe then agreed to a cup letter as the input (he has no tape underbust for B, so a letter, not an underbust, is the field): **the measurements file takes an optional top-level `"cup"` (`AA`, `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `DD`)**, mapped to the macro by `spike/cup_sizes.json` (`pack/cup.py`, tested by `pack/test_cup.py`). B's file says `"cup": "A"`, which is macro 0.1, so her body is unchanged. A file with both `cup` and `shape.cupsize` is refused. Files without `cup` build exactly as before. The letter is a stated fact (Joe's), not a tape measurement, and is not in `real`.
- **The table is not a tape standard.** The build does not aim for a bust-minus-underbust difference (A is 2.5 cm on paper): the macro cannot go below about 9 cm on B, and above macro 0.5 the difference stops tracking the cup. The letters are spaced by projection measured on B (macro 0 gives 1.05 cm, 0.1 1.2, 0.3 1.7, 0.5 3.2, 0.7 4.4): AA 0, A 0.1, B 0.3, C 0.45, D 0.6, DD 0.7. **Only A has been checked by eye; B to DD are untested placeholders** until someone with that cup is built.
- **The bust line must not follow the cup.** `body_from_measurements.py` finds the chest landmark as the widest point of the torso. With a small cup that point jumped up the ribs (112.8 cm instead of 110.5 cm on B), which would have moved the tape position, the tee's calibration line and the hang. The landmarks are now found at the default cup and held, then the real cup is set. At the default cup nothing changes.
- **One side effect, fixed.** A smaller bust calibrated the tee to a smaller ease (2.53 cm against 2.92 cm) and the back of the tee met the trousers' waistband (56 face pairs, the tee, V and scoop against trousers and shorts). Rather than force an ease floor, which would have made the tee bigger than the real size, the hung tee is rebuilt 0.6 cm clear of the trousers instead of 0.4 cm (`fit_config.json` `bust.tee_trousers_gap_cm`, applied to the tee and its V and scoop on female bodies only). Face pairs are 0 again. Tested at ease floors of 2.7 and 2.9 cm and at the 0.6 cm gap; all gave 0, and the gap keeps the size honest.
- **A number for the cup.** `bust_shape.body_cup` and the report's `bust_shape.body_cup` and `cupsize_macro`; `check_reports.py` prints them as an information line. They are reported, not limited, because no cup limit has been agreed.

## Other female bodies

The cause is shared: the three synthetic female bodies (and every woman built without a `cupsize`) get MPFB's average cup, which reads 14.5 to 15.5 cm bust minus underbust and 3.6 to 3.9 cm projection, much more than an A. I left them alone: they are test fixtures with no stated cup, their visual baselines are approved, and the shared code does what it did. Their garments changed only by the 2 mm wider tee clearance (tee-over-trousers size +1.3 cm at the waistband); their reports are regenerated. Male bodies and Joe's garments do not read the bust block, and the male synthetic reports differ only in the config hash.

## Known limits

- **Bust minus underbust is 9.9 cm, not the 2.5 cm of a textbook A cup.** The cup macro bottoms out at about 8.9 cm at the cup 0 setting, because the tape bust is fixed at 91.44 cm and the solver makes up the circumference with the ribcage and torso. B's real underbust is not yet in her file (V2-01 lists it as a measurement); when it arrives the build can be held to it, and `projection_cm` is the number that matches what Joe sees.
- **An underbust measurement would be better than a letter.** When B's real underbust exists, the solver could hit it with MPFB's `measure-underbust-circ` target and pick the cup from the tape. That is a later, separate change.
- B's hips are still a placeholder, as in V2-08.
