## How the tights are made

The trousers' template as a skin-close offset shell (`pack/fit_config.json`, `tights`):

- **2.5 mm off the skin** (`gap_cm`). The checks need 2 mm. The shorts sit about 3 mm off, so the two layers have half a millimetre between them.
- **4 smoothing passes**, not the trousers' 25. Heavy smoothing pulled the ankle out towards the wider foot (ankle 1.8 cm too big).
- **Hem at 8 cm** above the floor (the ankle; shoes arrive in v5). **Top 1 cm below** the shorts' waistband: with both edges level, they crossed on the large female body.
- **The shorts are built over the tights** and move out only where the two would touch (`extra_trigger` 0.6 mm, `extra_push` 1.2 mm; the tee over the trousers keeps its 1.5 and 3 mm). Every top still clears the shorts.
- **Then the tights are tucked inside the shorts** (`garment.tuck_inside`). The method uses the same face-pair overlap test as the checks, run on the exact triangles that get exported. It presses only the crossing tights triangles towards the skin, and never closer than 2.03 mm. A last pass keeps the true nearest-point clearance at 2.03 mm, which matters in the buttock cleft.

## What failed on the way (so nobody repeats it)

- **Nearest-point tests alone.** At the front of the crotch on the medium female body, every vertex of both garments was on its own side, but a coarse shorts triangle still cut through the tights. Changing margins never moved the count (8 pairs each time).
- **Testing the quads Blender holds.** The glTF exporter splits quads along its own diagonal, and the checks read the export. The fix: fit against Blender's loop triangles and triangulate the tights before tucking.
- **Snapping every tights vertex to exactly the gap.** It grabbed the foot and the groin (clearance -0.3 cm).
- **Building the shorts over the tights with the tee's 1.5/3 mm margins.** The shorts moved out up to 3 mm and the tees cut through them (12 to 217 pairs).
- **A smaller centre-plane gap for the tights than the shorts.** That puts the tights on the wrong side of the shorts' inner wall in the groin.

## What changed for existing garments

- **The shorts moved slightly** where they met the tights, on every body including Joe and B. Their minimum clearance to the body rose (for example 0.32 to 0.42 cm on synthetic_medium). ## Data model

- `tights` is a new garment id in a new **Legs** slot (`garment: 'tights'`, auto-named "Black tights" or "Black sheer tights").
- Saved items gain an optional `sheer` (tights only; `true` sheer, `false` opaque). No record version bump, because no older record holds tights.
- Outfits gain an optional `legs` item id. Older outfits load unchanged.
- The Dress screen's sheerness is stored under `sheer`, and is included in backups.
- Layering rule (`keepLayers` in `app/src/wardrobe.ts`): tights and trousers are never worn together. On Dress the Tights option is greyed out under trousers, with a note saying why. Putting trousers on takes the tights off. Wearing tights from the wardrobe takes trousers off.
- Sheer is drawn at 45% opacity (`SHEER_OPACITY` in `app/src/viewer.ts`), about a 20-denier look. It is a guess to tune by eye with B.
