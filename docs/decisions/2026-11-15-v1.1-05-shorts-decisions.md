# V1.1-05: shorts, above the knee, 23 cm inseam

Date: 2026-11-15. Ticket: `docs/tickets/v1.1-make-it-mine/V1.1-05-shorts.md`.

## Decision (Joe, 15 Nov 2026)

- **Length:** above the knee, 23 cm inseam (crotch to hem), the same on every body. One length for now. Set in `pack/fit_config.json` under `shorts.inseam_cm`.

## What I chose without asking (shout if you disagree)

- **Wider opening:** the leg opening is the trousers' own circumference at the shorts' hem height plus 4 cm (`leg_opening_extra_cm`), about 0.6 cm more room all round. On the synthetic medium body that is 57.5 cm against the trousers' 53.5 cm.
- **Only the bottom is let out:** the extra room fades in over the bottom 8 cm (`flare_band_cm`). Above that the shorts use the trousers' calibrated ease exactly, so the waist, hips and seat match the trousers, and the tee, V-neck, scoop and sweatshirt, which are built over the trousers, sit over the shorts the same way.
- **A separate file:** `shorts.glb` sits beside `trousers.glb` in the pack. Shorts have their own colour, like any other garment.

## Data model

- `shorts` is a new garment id for saved items (`garment: 'shorts'`, Bottom slot, auto-named 'Navy shorts'). No record version change: the record shape is the same, and older apps never stored shorts.

## Checks

- Shorts get the trousers' hard mesh limits (`spike/tolerances.json`, `garment_mesh`): one piece, three openings, no body through cloth, no self-intersection, minimum clearance.
- Every top is checked for intersections with the shorts as well as the trousers (tee-with-trousers limit, 0).
- Inseam, leg opening, waist and hips are compared with their targets within the garment landmark tolerance. `spike/check_reports.py --shorts` fails the pack build if the shorts are missing or miss a limit.
