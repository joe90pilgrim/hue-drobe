# V2-08 The tops no longer come to a point at each breast

Date: 16 November 2026. Result: **fixed on B and on the three synthetic female bodies, by a number and by eye.** Every top on a female body now hangs from the bust instead of tucking under it, every hard mesh check passes on all eight bodies, and Joe's and the male synthetic bodies' garments are byte-identical to before. Joe agreed the "no point" limits below. Not done: B's own visual sign-off.

## What the point was

The breast itself was not the problem. On B the exported tee's surface at the apex was already about as round as the pack dress that looked right by eye in V2-07. The point came from the cloth *under* the breast: the shell rests 3 mm off the body everywhere, and under each breast the body falls back 3 cm within 6 cm, so the cloth followed it in and rose steeply to the apex from below. From the front and the side that steep underside read as a cone. All seven V2-03 attempts changed the bust (nipple targets, smoothing, a bra) and so fought the chest calibration; none touched the cloth below it.

A first pass of the measuring script read the back of the body (glTF faces +Z; the sign was wrong), which is why the first depth maps looked flat. The numbers below come from the corrected maps.

## The measure (agreed by Joe, 16 Nov 2026)

`pack/bust_shape.py`, numpy only, on the exported GLBs. The body's apex is the most forward point of each breast within 4 cm of the bust landmark and 5 to 16 cm off the centre line; the top's apex is its most forward point within 2 cm sideways and 3 cm up or down of that. Per breast, in cm:

| Number | Meaning | Limit (`spike/tolerances.json` `bust_shape`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hollow_below | how far the cloth falls back behind its apex in the 6 cm below it (the tuck) | 0.7 |
| drop_up | how far it falls back 3 cm above the apex | 1.0 |
| drop_in | how far it falls back 3 cm towards the centre | 1.0 |
| drop_out | 3 cm away from the centre (the side of the chest, so reported only) | none |

1 cm over 3 cm is a radius of about 4.5 cm; a rounded cup is 6 to 8. For scale, the V2-07 pack dress on B measured hollow 1.0 to 1.1, drop up 0.75, drop in 0.35; Joe's tee (no apex to speak of) measures 0 to 0.4 on all four. The generator writes the numbers into every female body's report under `bust_shape`, and `spike/check_reports.py` fails a female body that misses a limit.

## The fix

`garment.build_tee` gained a `bust` option, used for the tee, its V and scoop variants and the sweatshirt, on a female body only (`sex_base`), with its settings in `pack/fit_config.json` under `bust`:

- **Hang.** Below each apex, down to 12 cm, a front-facing torso vertex is kept no further back than the cloth above it in its own column (within 2.5 cm sideways), less 0.05 cm per cm of drop. The floor tapers back by 1 cm per cm beyond the breast's own width, and falls away at 1 in 2 over the bottom third of the reach, so the hang ends in a slope at the sides and the bottom rather than a step. It is applied inside every push-out of the smoothing loop, so the smoothing blends it, and it only moves cloth away from the body, so it cannot cause a clip.
- **Size first, then drape.** The hang is drape, not size. Calibrating the chest on the hung cloth read up to 4 cm big at the bust line (with the first, wider hang), cut the tee's ease from 2.9 cm to 0.9 cm and put every top through the trousers' waistband (188 face pairs). So the ease and sleeve are settled as before, without the hang, and the shell is built once more at that ease with it. The report keeps `chest_circ_cm` as the garment's size and adds `chest_circ_as_worn_cm` for the hung mesh; the neckline variants compare their as-worn chest with the crew tee's.
- **Pad.** An optional extra clearance round each apex (a bra cup's rounding) is in the code but set to 0: the hang alone met the limits.

Two settings were found by measurement, not guessed: the column half-width had to be at least the mesh spacing (at 1.5 cm the chain of vertices broke and the cloth sagged back 1.5 cm on the medium synthetic body), and the hang had to stop at the breast's width (without the side taper the smoothing spread it into a flat wall right round under the arms).

## Known limits

- **The as-worn chest reads 0.7 to 1.3 cm over the garment's size.** The shell does not conserve cloth, so hanging the front forward adds to the section at the bust line. A real tee tube cannot grow; this is the offset-shell method's usual limit (B22), now visible at the bust.
- **The navel still shows through the tee** on B. Outside this ticket; it would take the same hang or a pad on the belly, or a smoothed fit body.
- **B's hips are still a placeholder.** Her apex is 1.5 cm behind her belly at the centre line, which is why the apex search stays 5 cm or more off the centre. Recheck the bust numbers when her tape hips arrive, as the ticket already asks for the trousers.
- The bust block is keyed on `sex_base`. A male chest might benefit from a hang too, but the ticket required the male garments unchanged.
