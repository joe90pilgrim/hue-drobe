## Why the belly stood out, and what changed

The generator's solver printed nothing about its fine controls, so I added a `SOLVER` line to the build log. For the build at the lowered waist, before any shape change, it showed the tape numbers were met by extreme settings: weight macro 1.0 (its ceiling), waist control 0.98 of 1.0, hips control -0.69 and, as the fallback for a waist the base body could not reach, the `stomach-pregnant` target at 0.28. That fallback is what pushed the belly forward. Raising the placeholder hips did not remove it.

I changed one thing at a time on scratch copies (one build each, Blender run directly, nothing committed) and measured the torso cross-section from each exported file. Waist depth is front to back, at the waist landmark; "bulge" is how far the front of the waist sits ahead of the front of the bust.

For comparison J's own body has a waist depth of 23.0 cm, a ratio of 0.81 and a bulge of 2.2 cm.

- **Chosen: `torso-scale-horiz` at +0.7.** The waist is now met by a wider torso instead of the belly target. Waist depth falls 2.4 cm and the bulge past the bust from 2.8 to 1.5 cm. The solver is no longer pinned at its weight ceiling (0.91), and nothing is at its limit. Going further gains under 0.3 cm and starts flattening the bust.
- **Not chosen: the `proportions` macro at 0.** It flattened the waist most (bulge 0.8 cm), but it is a whole-body setting, and its other effects (limb and head proportions) are not measured. Say if you want to try it.
- **Not chosen: a lower fixed weight.** The solver then leans harder on the belly target, which is the wrong way.

## What is not done

1. **B's sign-off.** There are no photos of B in `private/photos/`. The acceptance criterion is B's visual check against her photos, front and side. I looked at front, side and back myself. The belly is reduced but not gone: side view still shows a rounded front at the waist (1.5 cm past the bust). That is what the base body gives at this height and these three numbers.
2. **Hips are a placeholder (98 cm).** Hips came from the earlier placeholder file. Hips matter most to the overall proportion: at 98 cm the hips control sits at -0.53, so the base body is being narrowed there. Her tape hips are needed.
3. **Underbust was not added.** The ticket asks for bust and underbust in the solver. Bust is already solved: for a female body the `chest_cm` landmark is the bust (`measure-bust-circ`). Underbust has no tape value, so there is nothing to check it against. Thigh and inside leg were not supplied either.
4. **The app cannot show B yet.** The app and its service worker load only `pack/joe/`, and the person switch is V2-06. I viewed B in the spike viewer at `/?m=b&hide=tee,trousers` (the file is copied to `spike/viewer/public/models/personal/b.glb`, git-ignored). No frame rate was measured on the phone.

## Decisions I made (say if any is wrong)

1. **An optional `"shape"` block in a measurements file.** It holds proportions that tape measurements do not pin down: macros (`muscle`, `proportions`, `cupsize`, `firmness`), a fixed `weight`, or any signed MPFB target pair such as `torso-scale-horiz`. They are chosen by eye and listed in the report as `shape_by_eye`. The solver still sets the measured circumferences itself, so it refuses `shape` keys it controls. B's file uses `torso-scale-horiz: 0.7` only. This is a new input to the body build, so it touches the data model: **please confirm you want it kept.** J's file has no `shape` block and his build is unchanged.
2. **`"body_only": true` in a measurements file.** B's garments fail 10 checks with the default fit from `pack/fit_config.json` (checked on the first build; not re-run since the shape change): the trousers waist (5.1 cm over the 2 cm tolerance, which also fails the trousers and shorts waist comparisons), the tee_v calibration, and 7 face pairs of the tee and its neckline variants through the trousers and shorts. They belong to V2-03 and the open decision on fit per person. But `build_pack.py` writes no pack unless every check passes, and a full `--private` run would have refused to write J's pack because of B. So the flag makes the build, the checker and the pack judge and ship B's body alone. Nothing else is relaxed: no tolerance changed, and the synthetic bodies and J never use it. **Delete the line in V2-03.** A wider torso will change her garment fit again, so V2-03 starts from a fresh run.
3. **`PEOPLE = ["joe", "b"]` in `pack/build_pack.py`** replaces the hard-coded J, and `spike/build_all.py --private` now builds `b` instead of the old file name. A person whose file is missing is skipped.
4. **Left in place:** the older placeholder file in `private/` (git-ignored; I was not allowed to delete it, and the build no longer reads it) and its old build outputs in `private/out/`. Scratch bodies from the tweak tests are in the git-ignored viewer folder (`b_h*.glb`, `bv*.glb`, `bw1.glb`, `bx*.glb`). J can delete them.
5. **Not renamed:** earlier docs and tickets still use B's full name. J asked me not to use it; I have not touched the old files. Say if you want them changed.
6. **New dependency:** none. `measure_glb.py` uses numpy, already installed; it is a local script and not part of the app.
