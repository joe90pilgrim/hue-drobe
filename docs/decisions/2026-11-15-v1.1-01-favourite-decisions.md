# V1.1-01: favourites can be any colour, and show the first 12

Date: 2026-11-15. Ticket: `docs/tickets/v1.1-make-it-mine/V1.1-01-favourite-colours.md`.

## Decision

Joe chose the recommended options for the two open questions:

- **Off-palette colours:** yes. A colour picked freely in the colour picker can be saved as a favourite. Each favourite records whether it came from the quiz palette, and the picker marks palette colours.
- **Limit:** none. The picker shows the first 12 favourites and a 'more' option.

## Why

Favourites are the link between the quiz and the outfit screen. Limiting them to the palette would stop Joe keeping a colour he already owns that the quiz did not suggest. A 12-swatch view keeps the picker usable on a phone.

## How it is stored

IndexedDB key `favourites`, a list of `{ hex, fromPalette }` in the order they were saved. Each hex is lower-case `#rrggbb`, with no duplicates.
