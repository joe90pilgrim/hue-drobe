# V1.1-03: preset means saved outfits; applying replaces everything

Date: 2026-11-15. Ticket: `docs/tickets/v1.1-make-it-mine/V1.1-03-preset-outfits.md`.

## Decision

- **What 'preset' means:** outfits you save yourself. Starter outfits that come with the app are not in this version; they are on the roadmap as idea 17, to consider later.
- **Applying an outfit:** replaces everything on the avatar. A slot the outfit leaves empty is cleared to None.

## How it is stored and behaves

- IndexedDB key `outfits`, a list of `{ v: 1, id, name, top, bottom, savedAt }`. `top` and `bottom` are the ids of saved items (see `docs/decisions/2026-11-15-v1.1-02-saved-clothes-decisions.md`).
- Saving a look from the Dress screen saves each garment on the avatar in the colour the picker shows. A colour already in the library is reused, as the Save button does; a new one is added to the library. So saving an outfit can add items to the library.
- Names are auto-generated from the items (for example 'Navy tee, Light grey trousers') and editable, as for saved items. This was not in the ticket's decision list; it follows the saved-clothes decision.
- If an item is deleted, the outfit keeps its id and shows that slot as empty. Applying it clears that slot.
